## Source Outcomes

- Six JD products produced current detail images and OCR.
- Chanel product 121 returned no public JD description and was rejected.
- YSL Taiwan official product page exposed usable product text.
- Lancome China official search result exposed an image-heavy shell without usable product text.
- La Roche-Posay China official search result exposed only a site-level regulatory notice, not the target product body.

## Conclusion

- Current retail detail pages are effective for soft decision fields and usage.
- Brand official pages vary sharply in extractability.
- Official product descriptions are still claims, not hard safety evidence.
- Registration and verified full-package sources remain necessary for ingredients, verified absences, and safety hard filters.
